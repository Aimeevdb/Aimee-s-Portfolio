I'm building something I actually need: ADHD Quest, a task app that turns "I have to do this" into a quest you complete with a tiny wizard gnome cheering you on.

If you have ADHD, you know the problem isn't laziness — it's that tasks feel too big, too vague, and too unrewarding to start. ADHD Quest breaks tasks into small steps, celebrates every one, and closes the loop with a real reward instead of just a checked box.

Right now I'm in the early build-in-public phase: the first interactive prototype is live in Figma, and I just launched a short usability survey to see if the core flow actually clicks for people before I go further.

Two ways to help:
🧙 Try the prototype (5 screens, 2 minutes)
📋 Take the survey (5 minutes, brutally honest feedback welcome)
